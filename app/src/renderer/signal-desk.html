Welcome to BetterFingers

BetterFingers turns your voice into text anywhere on your system — fully local, no cloud. This quick setup takes about a minute.

What you'll do

  • Review how BetterFingers uses your data
  • Learn the record → review → send flow
  • Make sure a speech model is installed

BetterFingers runs a local speech-to-text, rewrite, and text-to-speech pipeline on your own machine. Audio you record is transcribed locally and is not sent to any external server.

The only network activity is downloading the AI models you choose to install. You can delete recordings, drafts, and models at any time from the app.

By continuing you confirm you will use BetterFingers lawfully and only to capture speech you are authorized to capture.

  • Record — press your record hotkey (or use the tray) and speak. Hold-to-talk and press-to-toggle are both supported.
  • Review — a draft appears with the cleaned-up text. Rewrite it (Clearer / Shorter / Tone), edit inline, or have it read back to you.
  • Send — accept to type or paste it into whatever app you're in.

You can change hotkeys, injection behavior, and more under Settings.

A speech model is installed — you're ready to go. You can manage or add models any time from Utilities → Models.

No speech model is installed yet. Open Utilities → Models to download the recommended set for your hardware (a small Whisper model for transcription, plus an optional local LLM for cleanup). You can finish setup now and download models whenever you're ready.
