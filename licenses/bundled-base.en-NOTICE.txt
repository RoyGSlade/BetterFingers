BetterFingers bundles the following speech-recognition model:

  Systran/faster-whisper-base.en, revision 3d3d5dee26484f91867d81cb899cfcf72b96be6c
  https://huggingface.co/Systran/faster-whisper-base.en
  A conversion by SYSTRAN of OpenAI's Whisper base.en weights to the
  CTranslate2 format. Files: config.json, model.bin, tokenizer.json,
  vocabulary.txt (sizes and SHA-256 in backend/stt/bundled_manifest.json).
