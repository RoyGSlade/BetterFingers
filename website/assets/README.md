# Project assets

The reviewed BetterFingers product tour lives in `website/assets/showcase/`
and is declared by `website/project.json`. Its five scenes use current
private-alpha QA captures rather than mockups or remote media:

- the complete Speak, Review, Deliver workflow composite;
- focused Speak, Review, and Deliver crops;
- Persona Studio editing and testing views;
- the full-width recoverable Library view; and
- local model, privacy, and data controls.

The ordinary `screenshots` collection remains empty. Onboarding can be added
there later, or promoted to a sixth showcase scene after its media is reviewed.
Keep the `Product in development` label until the repository's release evidence
supports changing it. Do not replace these captures with placeholders,
unreviewed UI, private footage, or media that overstates an unfinished feature.
